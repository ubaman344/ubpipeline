Questions founders ask

Clear before
we start.

Will the content sound like me?

That is the goal. We begin by learning how you speak, think, disagree, explain, and tell stories. You approve the content until the voice feels unmistakably yours.

How involved do I need to be?

Expect a focused onboarding and short recurring input. UB Pipeline handles the day-to-day execution, while you provide the insight only you can provide.

Are the comments and DMs automated?

Our positioning is human-led and relevance-first. We do not want your name attached to generic comments, copied outreach, or conversations that damage trust.

Do you guarantee leads or sales?

We do not promise a fixed number of leads or sales because results also depend on your offer, market, and sales process. What we do commit to is consistent execution: stronger positioning, seven weekly posts, daily relationship building, relevant outreach, and continuous improvement, all designed to create more qualified conversations and a healthier pipeline.

What does it cost?

Every engagement is scoped privately around your current LinkedIn presence, target market, publishing needs, and level of relationship management. After reviewing your profile and goals, we recommend a clear scope and fee so you know exactly what UB Pipeline will handle and how the work is designed to support a return.
